Why Zreta exists

A shared commercial and trust layer for modular industry products.

What we are building

Zreta.com markets, bills, and supports modular products. Live products run as their own applications; Zreta provides the storefront, portal, documentation, and shared security practices.

Where we operate

Africa-first commercial packaging — GHS pricing and Mobile Money gateways — with products useful beyond the region.

Engineering philosophy

Prefer modular live products over one monolith claiming every engine. Publish Live vs Coming soon honestly.

Security philosophy

Surface real controls — MFA, audit logs, private payment proofs, status — instead of unverifiable global trust claims. See the Security Center.

Company facts

What a buyer can verify without a sales call.

What we sell

Subscriptions and access to modular products via zreta.com, with shared billing, customer portal, documentation, and support tooling.

Live products today

ChurchHub and CoreTrust. Roadmap products (ERP, School, Hospital, HR) are labelled Coming soon until GA.

What is external

ChurchHub and CoreTrust run as separate product applications. Zreta does not claim those engines live inside this marketing monolith.

How to reach us

Use Contact, the Support Center, or published security/privacy addresses on those pages.
